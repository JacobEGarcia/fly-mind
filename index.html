METHOD / SOURCES

A map, made explorable.

This study renders soma positions for 139,255 neurons and 743,322 strong connections, defined here as 12 or more synapses, from FlyWire FAFB v783. Dark points and lines show mapped structure. Specimen red shows activity seeded by the selected circuit.

Activity is an interpretive network-propagation model for exploring structure. It is not a biological prediction, a claim of consciousness, or a brain upload. Data comes from the FlyWire community reconstruction. See Dorkenwald et al., Nature 2024, and Schlegel et al., 2024. Built by Jacob Garcia, September 2026.
