Not a brain in a cockpit.

The visualization asks a narrower question: what might happen if known circuit categories were translated into a tiny flight controller? Visual activity damps drift. Descending activity changes heading. Motor drive changes thrust. The mappings are designed for this exhibit and are not predictions of an animal’s neural code.
