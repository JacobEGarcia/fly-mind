Three behavioral arenas translate approach, indecision, and avoidance into an editorial triptych. “Hell,” “Purgatory,” and “Heaven” are human exhibit labels. The underlying scientific frame is aversive, neutral, and appetitive response.

AVERSIVE ARENA

Hell

Modeled heat, threat, and bitter cues increase avoidance and escape probability.

NEUTRAL ARENA

Purgatory

Competing weak signals produce searching, pausing, and no strong directional preference.

APPETITIVE ARENA

Heaven

Modeled sugar and attractive odor cues increase approach and dwelling probability.

Purgatory / searching

No circuit response is a moral state or an afterlife. The neutral model holds approach and avoidance near balance, producing exploratory movement. Choose an arena to compare the model.
