▼ THE SCIENCE + THE DATA

In March 2026, Eon Systems announced the first embodied whole-brain emulation: a copy of a fruit fly's brain, wired neuron-to-neuron from real electron-microscopy data, driving a physics-simulated body — walking, grooming, feeding (The Register, open-source model).

The wiring comes from FlyWire: 139,255 neurons and ~50 million synapses, proofread by thousands of gamers and scientists (Dorkenwald et al., Nature 2024). A 2024 Nature model (Shiu et al.) predicted the real fly's neural activity with ~91% accuracy from connectivity alone.

This piece renders the real soma positions of all 139,255 neurons and the real 743,322 strongest synaptic connections (≥12 synapses each). Coloring is by cell class; pulses travel the actual wiring. The fly below reacts to activity in its real descending and motor neurons.
